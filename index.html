Our Story

An Old-School Gem in the Heart of Chowk

One of the oldest and most loved snack points in old Lucknow, still serving the flavours the city grew up with.

Serving Chowk Since 1983

Shri Lassi Corner has been serving in Chowk, Lucknow since 1983. Set on Kamla Nehru Marg near Gol Darwaza, the small shop sits in one of the city’s most historic food quarters, surrounded by the lanes and bazaars of old Lucknow.

As the name promises, the lassi is the star here — thick, creamy and refreshing, finished with a generous topping of kesar malai. Regulars pair it with the shop’s other claim to fame: piping-hot chole bhature, with fluffy, freshly fried bhature and dark, richly spiced chole.

The setup is simple and the seating basic, in the true old-Lucknow way — but the food, the quick service and the familiar taste are exactly why people keep coming back, decade after decade.

Signature & Menu Highlights

The Tastes People Come Back For

The documented house specialities, made fresh at the counter through the day — no long lists, just the classics done right.

Signature

Kesar Malai Lassi

The house signature: thick, creamy lassi crowned with kesar malai — rich, refreshing and best enjoyed chilled, in the classic Chowk style. Ask for it in a kulhad for the traditional earthen touch.

House Favourite

Chole Bhature

The other half of the shop’s fame: soft, fluffy bhature that balloon up fresh from the kadhai, served with dark, spicy chole cooked in the house masala — a plate regulars call the real taste of Lucknow.

Traditional Favourite

Badam Milk

Warm, comforting badam milk — a traditional favourite at the counter, especially loved in the cooler months alongside the shop’s sweets.
